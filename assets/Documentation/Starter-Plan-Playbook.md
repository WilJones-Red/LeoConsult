# Starter Plan Playbook

**Internal + Client-Facing Document**  
**Leo Consult**

---

## Purpose

The Starter Plan is our entry-level analytics subscription designed to:

- Give small businesses access to professional data analytics
- Build foundational BI dashboards that serve real decisions
- Establish data quality practices from the start
- Create monthly reporting that drives action
- Build trust through consistent, decision-focused delivery

This is an ongoing monthly service, not a one-time project. It provides continuous analytics support for growing businesses.

---

## Pricing

**$1,500 per month**

Monthly subscription with no long-term contract required. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

---

## What the Starter Plan Includes

**Basic BI Dashboards**

Dashboards built around the decisions your business needs to make. Clean, focused, and usable.

**Monthly Performance Reports**

Written analysis of what the data is telling you and what decisions it supports. Plain language, no jargon.

**Data Quality Assessment**

Ongoing monitoring and documentation of data quality issues. We tell you when the foundation is not trustworthy.

**Up to 3 Data Sources**

Integration with three key data sources (CRM, accounting system, analytics platform, etc.).

**Email Support**

Responsive email support for questions, clarifications, and guidance.

**Monthly Optimization Reviews**

Regular check-ins to ensure dashboards and reports stay relevant as your business evolves.

---

## What the Starter Plan Does Not Include

**Advanced predictive analytics or machine learning**

These are available in the Growth Plan ($3,500/month). Starter focuses on foundational BI.

**Real-time dashboards**

Starter includes standard reporting. Real-time capabilities require Growth Plan.

**Unlimited data sources**

Starter supports up to 3 data sources. Additional sources require Growth or Enterprise plans.

**Priority or 24/7 support**

Starter includes email support during business hours. Priority support is available in higher tiers.

**Dedicated data science team**

Starter clients work with our analytics team on a shared basis. Dedicated teams are Enterprise-level.

**White-glove onboarding**

Starter includes standard onboarding (2-3 weeks). Enterprise clients receive comprehensive white-glove service.

## How to Explain the Starter Plan to Clients

Use this language in conversations:

> "The Starter Plan is $1,500 per month and includes basic BI dashboards, monthly performance reports, data quality monitoring, and support for up to three data sources.
>
> The focus is on building dashboards that serve actual decisions, not decorative analytics. We start by understanding what decisions you need to make, then build reporting around those.
>
> Implementation takes 2-3 weeks. After that, you get monthly reports and ongoing support. You can upgrade to Growth Plan anytime if you need advanced analytics or real-time capabilities.
>
> No long-term contract. If it is not working, cancel with 30 days notice."

Key points to emphasize:

- Decision-focused dashboards, not dashboard theater
- Quick implementation (2-3 weeks to launch)
- Ongoing service with monthly reporting and support
- Clear upgrade path if needs grow
- No lock-in, cancel anytime

## Success Criteria

A successful Starter Plan engagement achieves:

**Client uses the dashboards regularly**

The dashboards are referenced in actual business decisions, not ignored.

**Monthly reports drive action**

The client can point to specific decisions or changes made based on the reports.

**Data quality issues are surfaced and understood**

The client knows where their data is trustworthy and where it needs work.

**Communication is working**

Client gets timely responses to questions and feels supported.

**Client retention**

Client continues the subscription because they are getting value, or upgrades to Growth/Enterprise.

**Clear upgrade trigger points**

Client understands when and why they might need advanced capabilities.

## Possible Outcomes and Upgrade Paths

**Outcome 1: Client stays on Starter Plan (most common)**

Their needs are met with basic BI and monthly reporting. They continue the subscription indefinitely.

**Outcome 2: Client upgrades to Growth Plan ($3,500/month)**

They need:
- Advanced predictive analytics or machine learning
- Real-time dashboards instead of monthly reports
- More than 3 data sources
- Weekly insights instead of monthly
- Priority support

**Outcome 3: Client upgrades to Enterprise (custom pricing)**

They need:
- Dedicated data science team
- Custom analytics solutions
- Advanced AI implementation
- White-glove service and 24/7 support
- Quarterly business reviews

**Outcome 4: Client cancels**

The fit was not right, timing was off, or they are not ready for analytics. We part ways respectfully with 30 days notice.

All outcomes are valid. Success is delivering value at the right level.

## Scope and Boundary Management

**Fixed monthly deliverables**

Starter Plan includes: dashboards (initially built), monthly reports, data quality monitoring, email support. No scope creep.

**3 data sources maximum**

If client wants more, they upgrade to Growth Plan. No exceptions.

**Monthly reporting cadence**

Starter gets monthly reports. If they want weekly, they upgrade to Growth.

**Email support only**

No priority support, no emergency calls. Business hours email response. Higher tiers get priority access.

**No custom development**

Starter uses standard analytics approaches. Custom ML models, advanced AI, or specialized solutions require Growth or Enterprise.

**Clear pricing**

$1,500/month, billed monthly. No hidden costs. Upgrades are transparent and optional.

## Internal Execution Timeline

**Week 1: Listen and Align**

- Free initial consultation to understand business and decisions
- Review what data they have and what they do not
- Identify quick wins and long-term opportunities
- Provide clear roadmap before work begins
- Client approves plan before implementation

**Weeks 2-3: Build**

- Fix critical data quality issues
- Build decision-focused dashboards (not decorative)
- Weekly check-ins so client knows progress
- Early access for review and feedback

**Week 3-4: Train and Launch**

- Hands-on training tailored to client team
- Plain-English documentation (no jargon)
- Recorded walkthroughs for reference
- Direct support channel established

**Ongoing: Support and Optimize**

- Monthly performance reports delivered
- Monthly optimization reviews to keep dashboards relevant
- Email support for questions
- Updates as business evolves

Typical launch timeline: 2-3 weeks from signup to fully operational.

## Common Questions

**What if the client wants features outside Starter scope?**

Explain clearly what tier includes that feature. Offer upgrade path. Do not bend scope to keep them at Starter pricing.

**What if the data is worse than expected?**

Pause implementation. Document issues clearly. Give client options: fix data first, adjust scope, or cancel. Do not build on bad data.

**What if the client stops using the dashboards?**

Reach out proactively. Understand why. Adjust if needed. If truly not a fit, help them cancel respectfully.

**What if the client wants to downgrade or cancel?**

Honor the 30-day notice period. Exit gracefully. Document lessons learned. This is expected and normal.

**What if client needs custom analytics but cannot afford Enterprise?**

Offer Growth Plan as middle ground. If truly cannot afford it, be honest that we may not be the right fit right now. Do not discount Enterprise work to starter pricing.

**What if we realize this is not a good fit during implementation?**

Be direct. Complete the current month as promised. Help them transition out. Refund if appropriate. Protect the relationship and reputation.
