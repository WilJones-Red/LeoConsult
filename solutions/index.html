Our Approach

Every solution starts with understanding the decision you need to make. We don't build dashboards for the sake of dashboards. We focus on data that helps you act with confidence.

How Leo Consult Works

A clear, data-driven process designed to solve your specific problem

1. Discovery Conversation

We start by understanding your business problem, not your data. What decision are you trying to make? What's the real challenge?

  • Identify the core business question
  • Understand current decision-making
  • Define success criteria
  • Assess data availability

2. Data Analysis & Discovery

We dig into your data to find insights that matter. Quality checks come first—we only report on data we can trust.

  • Data quality assessment
  • Exploratory analysis
  • Pattern identification
  • Opportunity quantification

3. Clear Recommendations

You get straightforward findings with honest assessments. We tell you what we found, what we're confident about, and where uncertainty remains.

  • Plain-language insights
  • Actionable recommendations
  • Clear next steps
  • Transparent limitations

4. Implementation Support

We don't just hand you a report and disappear. We help you act on the findings and measure results.

  • Action plan development
  • Team collaboration
  • Progress tracking
  • Ongoing refinement
