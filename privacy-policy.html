Privacy Policy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Leo Consult ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website leoconsult.org (the "Site").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Contact Forms: Name, email address, company name, phone number, and any message you provide
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Email address
  • ROI Calculator: Business metrics you input (not stored unless you submit a contact form)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically (with your consent)

  • Analytics Data: When you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to collect:
    • Pages visited and time spent on pages
    • Referral sources (how you found our site)
    • Device type, browser, and approximate location (city/country level)
    • IP address (anonymized)

2.3 Essential Cookies (No Consent Required)

  • Cookie Consent Preference: Remembers your cookie choice for 365 days

3. Cookie Policy

3.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

3.2 Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
leo_cookie_consent Stores your cookie preference (accepted/rejected) 365 days

Analytics Cookies (Requires Consent)

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics - Distinguishes users 2 years
_gid Google Analytics - Distinguishes users 24 hours
_gat Google Analytics - Throttles request rate 1 minute

3.3 Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent before setting analytics cookies. You can:

  • Accept: Allow analytics cookies to help us improve the site
  • Essential Only: Only use essential cookies needed for the site to function

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site, or by managing cookies in your browser settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To Respond to Inquiries: Answer your questions and provide requested information
  • To Improve Our Services: Analyze site usage to enhance user experience
  • To Send Updates: If you opt-in, send newsletters about our services and insights
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Google Analytics (with anonymized IP), email service providers
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

6. Data Retention

  • Contact Form Data: Retained for up to 2 years or until you request deletion
  • Analytics Data: Automatically deleted by Google Analytics after 26 months
  • Cookie Consent: Stored for 365 days

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw cookie consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@leoconsult.org

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

11. International Data Transfers

12. Changes to This Policy
